Interpretive guardrails

- GT-BEHRT paper states MIMIC-IV hosp-module conditions, medications, procedures and context before ICU admission; it does not enumerate the raw MIMIC table-to-code mapping or code standardization.
- HypeMed paper states MIMIC-IV v2.0, SafeDrug-style processing, low-frequency filtering, >=2 visits and standardized ICD/ATC codes; it does not publish the frequency threshold, raw table mapping, crosswalk details, or train/validation/test split ratio.
- HypeMed uses three separate domain hypergraphs. A visit induces one hyperedge per domain; node-hyperedge membership is the incidence relation. Do not describe this as an ordinary pairwise edge graph.
